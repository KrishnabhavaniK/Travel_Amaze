About Hyderabad

Hyderabad, the City of Nizams, is a perfect blend of history and modernity. Known for its iconic Charminar, delectable biryani, and rich cultural heritage, the city offers magnificent palaces, historic monuments, and vibrant bazaars.

Charminar

A historic monument and mosque, symbolizing the cultural heritage of Hyderabad.

  • Location: Old City, Hyderabad
  • Built in 1591 by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah
  • Surrounded by Laad Bazaar, famous for bangles & pearls

Golconda Fort

A majestic fort known for its acoustic system and rich history.

  • Location: Hyderabad
  • Unique acoustic architecture
  • Spectacular evening light & sound show

Hussain Sagar Lake

A heart-shaped lake with the iconic Buddha statue in the center.

  • Location: Hyderabad
  • Boating & ferry rides
  • Scenic walking trails & parks

Salar Jung Museum

A treasure trove of rare artifacts, antiques, and art from around the world.

  • Location: Hyderabad
  • Home to the famous Veiled Rebecca statue
  • 19th-century musical clock attraction

Ramoji Film City

One of the largest film studio complexes in the world, offering tours and entertainment.

  • Location: Hyderabad
  • Behind-the-scenes film production experience
  • Live shows & adventure rides
