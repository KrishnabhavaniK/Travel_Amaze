About Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is an ivory-white marble mausoleum on the right bank of the river Yamuna in Agra, India. It was commissioned in 1632 by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan to house the tomb of his favorite wife, Mumtaz Mahal.

Local Delicacies

Agra's Famous Dishes

  • Petha: Famous sweet made from ash gourd
  • Bedai: Stuffed kachori served with spicy potato curry
  • Paratha: Agra's famous stuffed flatbreads
  • Dalmoth: Spicy snack mix

Uttar Pradesh Specialties

  • Kebabs: Lucknowi Galouti and Tunday Kebabs
  • Chaat: Various street food delicacies
  • Biryani: Awadhi style rice preparation
  • Jalebi: Popular sweet dish

Pro Tip: The best time to try local street food is during evening hours when the shops are bustling with locals.
