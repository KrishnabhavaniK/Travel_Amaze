About Goa

Goa, India's smallest state, is a paradise of beautiful beaches, vibrant nightlife, Portuguese architecture, and delicious seafood. Known for its laid-back atmosphere and water sports, it's the perfect blend of relaxation and adventure.

Goan Specialties

  • Fish Curry Rice: Traditional Goan curry
  • Vindaloo: Spicy curry dish
  • Xacuti: Coconut-based curry

Local Favorites

  • Bebinca: Traditional layered dessert
  • Feni: Local cashew/coconut liquor
  • Prawn Balchao: Spicy prawn pickle
